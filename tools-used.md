# Tools Used in LUME Project

## Large Language Models (LLMs)
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** – Main model for content generation, strategy, and complex reasoning
- **GPT-4o** – Used for research, audience analysis, and quick iterations

## Automation & Integration Tools
- **Zapier** – Automated content distribution and data syncing
- **Make.com** – Built complex multi-step workflows and automations

## Design & Creative Tools
- **Midjourney** – AI image generation for social media content
- **Canva** – Final design adjustments and templates

## Analytics & Research
- **Perplexity AI** – Market and competitor research
- **LinkedIn** – Audience research and lead generation
- **Platform Analytics** – Performance tracking and insights

## Documentation & Organization
- **Notion** – Project planning, documentation, and knowledge base
- **Google Sheets** – Data tracking and performance analysis

---

*This project was built entirely using AI tools and automation with minimal manual intervention.*
