# LUME – AI-Powered Personal Brand

**LUME** is a personal brand I built entirely from scratch using advanced AI tools, prompt engineering, and automation.

## Why I Built It
I wanted to explore how AI can be used to create and grow a complete brand with minimal resources. The goal was to test whether well-designed AI systems can deliver real business results.

## What I Built
- Advanced prompt engineering workflows for content creation
- Automated marketing and distribution systems
- AI-powered audience research and strategy tools
- Self-improving feedback loops for continuous optimization

## Results
- Grew from **0 to 500+ followers** in just 2 months
- Generated **first sales** within the same period
- Proved that AI can be a powerful tool for building real-world projects

## What I Learned
- How to design reliable AI workflows
- The importance of iteration and testing
- How to combine multiple AI tools into a complete system

This project deepened my understanding of **prompt engineering**, **AI automation**, and **building production-ready AI systems**.
