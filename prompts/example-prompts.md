# Example Prompts Used in LUME

## 1. Content Generation Prompt: You are an expert health and wellness content creator.
Write a short, engaging Instagram caption (max 150 words) about [topic].
Tone: friendly, trustworthy, and slightly inspirational.
Include 3-5 relevant emojis and a call-to-action at the end.

## 2. Audience Research Prompt: Analyze the target audience for a premium 3D-prined LED lamps brand targeting [age group] in Croatia.
List their main pain points, desires, and preferred content formats.
Provide 5 content ideas that would resonate with this audience.

## 3. Email Sequence Prompt: Write a 4-email welcome sequence for new subscribers of a 3D-prined LED lamps brand.
Each email should be under 200 words. Focus on building trust and gently introducing the product.

## 4. Performance Analysis Prompt: Analyze the following social media performance data and suggest 3 specific improvements
to increase engagement and follower growth: [paste data here].

*Note: These are example prompts. Actual prompts used in production were more detailed and context-specific.*
